HOW EUROPEAN ODDS WORK

Educational guide for sport administration students

1. European Decimal Odds Explained

In European sports betting, the odds represent the total payout multiplier for every 1 unit staked.

Total Return = Stake × Decimal Odds
Net Profit = (Stake × Decimal Odds) - Stake

For example: A bet of 100 Squid placed at odds of 1.47 pays out 147.00 Squid (a net profit of +47.00 Squid).

2. Converting Probability to Decimal Odds

European Decimal Odds are mathematically the reciprocal of implied market probability:

  • • 68% probability = 1 / 0.68 = 1.47 odds
  • • 50% probability = 1 / 0.50 = 2.00 odds
  • • 32% probability = 1 / 0.32 = 3.12 odds
